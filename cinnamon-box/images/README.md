# Shot list — Sweet Dough weekend cinnamon box reel

Drop the four files in this folder with these exact names. JPEG or PNG, sRGB,
no text or logos baked in. Shoot them in the same warm daylight so they cut
together.

---

## 1. `tray.jpg` — the hook (0.0–3.0s)

- **Crop:** vertical 9:16. Minimum **1620 × 2880**.
- **Use:** full-bleed background behind "WEEKEND CINNAMON BOX". Darkened to roughly
  65–85% and slowly zoomed in 1.14× over the scene.
- **Subject:** a tray or sheet pan of cinnamon rolls coming out of the oven. This is
  the scroll-stopper, so it needs motion and heat — steam, a hand on the pan, icing
  going on.
- **Composition:** keep the rolls in the **top half**. The bottom half is covered by
  the headline and a dark gradient.
- **Because it zooms:** shoot wider than you think and leave a little margin.

## 2. `box.jpg` — the product (3.0–7.0s, and on the phone screen 8.8–12.0s)

- **Crop:** vertical 4:5. Minimum **2000 × 2500**.
- **Use:** cropped **square** in the box scene, and again **3:4** inside the phone
  mockup. One photo, two crops — so centre the box and leave generous margin on all
  four sides.
- **Subject:** the open kraft box with the six rolls, shot top-down (flat lay) on a
  clean surface.
- **This is the hero shot.** If only one photo gets proper attention, make it this one.
- **Avoid:** props crowding the corners — a "Weekends only" sticker sits over the
  top-right corner of the frame.

## 3. `roll.jpg` — the detail (7.0–8.8s)

- **Crop:** landscape 5:4. Minimum **2000 × 1600**.
- **Use:** sits under the "What's in the box" list on a cream background, with a slow
  1.1× zoom.
- **Subject:** macro close-up of a single roll pulled apart — the spiral, the pull,
  icing stretching. Shallow depth of field.
- **Note:** it lands on a light background, so a darker, richer exposure reads best.

## 4. `counter.jpg` — the end card (12.0–15.0s)

- **Crop:** vertical 9:16. Minimum **1620 × 2880**.
- **Use:** background behind the SWEET DOUGH wordmark, darkened to about 80% — it
  reads as texture, not as a photo.
- **Subject:** the shop counter or interior in morning light.
- **Must work very dark.** Keep it simple and low-contrast; busy detail turns to mud.
- **Keep the centre clear** — the wordmark and handle sit dead centre.
