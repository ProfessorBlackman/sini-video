# Real-assets round (v0.7): briefs

**Setup:** Sini 0.1.7 (`ghcr.io/professorblackman/sini:0.1`, local build of the release) through the MCP server only. The client is MedVerify, a real product of the owner's: a free Android app that looks a medicine up in Ghana's FDA register and reports its registration status ([landing page](https://medverify.versatechq.com/)).

**Goal:** the first round with real client material: a logo (`logo.webp`) and three phone screenshots, two of them taller than the screen (`home.png` 390×884, `manual-search.png` 390×884, `scan-result.png` 390×1317). And a real copy constraint: MedVerify reports *registration*, never that a medicine is *safe* or *genuine*, yet the scan-result screenshot reads "Verified Safe".

| # | Project | Model | Format | Brief |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | `m1-launch` | default | 16:9, ~25s | Launch video for the landing page and LinkedIn: the problem (medicine sold on trotros and roadside trays; is it even registered?), the app (search by name, scan the barcode, or type what's on the pack), the result, then "Free · No account · No phone number" and "Get the app for Android", medverify.versatechq.com. Feedback round: "Show the logo at the start too, and hold the ending a bit longer so people can read the URL." |
| M2 | `m2-reel` | default | 9:16, ~15s | Reel for Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp status: hook question, the app in a phone (scan → result), the promise, the call to action. |
| M2h | `m2h-reel` | Haiku | 9:16, ~15s | Same as M2. |

Brand (from the site's design notes): forest `#0b5d3b`, deep `#0a4a30`, leaf `#17915a`, mint `#e7f5ed`, wash `#f3faf6`, ink `#0d2b20`, dim `#4f6259`; Plus Jakarta Sans for display, rounded cards (16px).
